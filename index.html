Typing is forgiving about form: vii°, viio, viiø, vii dim and 7° all mean the diminished triad; Dm, Dmin, d minor and D- all mean D minor. Accidentals may be typed as # or b, and enharmonic roots (D♭ for C♯) are accepted. Getting the letter-case wrong in a roman numeral still counts as correct, but you’ll get a note about it — the case is the part being taught.
